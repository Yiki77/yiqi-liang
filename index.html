Lingqi Yan - GAMES101

Introduction of Lingqi Yan:

Assistant Professor @ UCSB.

Course Introduction:

This course comprehensively and systematically introduces the four major components of modern computer graphics: (1) rasterization imaging, (2) geometric representation, (3) the theory of light propagation, and (4) animation and simulation. Each aspect is explained from basic principles to practical applications, along with the introduction of cutting-edge theoretical research. Through this course, you can learn the mathematics and physics behind computer graphics and enhance your practical programming skills. As an introduction, this course aims to cover as many aspects of graphics as possible, clearly explaining the basic concepts of each part to provide a complete, top-down understanding of computer graphics.

More details can be found in Seminar Home Page .

Introduction of Mu Li:

AWS Senior Chief Scientist, PhD in Computer Science, Carnegie Mellon University.

Introduction of Course:

Deep learning is a research field that has received much attention in recent years and plays an important role in machine learning. Deep learning can interpret external data by building and simulating the hierarchical structure of the human brain to extract features from low-level to high-level input data. The course introduces the foundations of deep learning, including linear regression, multilayer perceptrons, convolutional neural networks, recurrent neural networks, optimization algorithms, object detection, semantic segmentation, and style transfer.

VectorStyleDraw

Motivation:

VectorStyleDraw is an algorithm that synthesizes vector-style drawings based on an initial input image and a style image. Like CLIPDraw, VectorStyleDraw does not require additional training. It uses a pretrained CLIP language-image encoder to preserve semantic similarity to the input image and Earth Mover Distance as a style loss. VectorStyleDraw preserves both the characteristics of the initial image and the visual style of the style image.

MediSUM

Motivation:

Medication instructions often contain small fonts, complex language, and large amounts of information. Due to lower vision, memory decline, and related age-associated challenges, older adults may have greater difficulty reading and using medication information leaflets. Based on these problems, we designed MediSUM, a WeChat Mini Program that helps older adults read and understand medication information. MediSUM: 1) digitalizes medication instructions, 2) reorganizes their contents, and 3) integrates ChatGPT-based interactive assistance.

LayerTouch

Motivation:

Blind and low vision people often rely on alt text to understand images. Previous research has explored touchscreen-based image exploration as a way to supplement alt text and provide users with more detailed access to visual information. LayerTouch enables blind and low vision users to explore image objects according to different depth layers, providing structured touch-based access to image content.

Motivation:

VectorDraw is an algorithm that synthesizes vector drawings from different input images. We present five types of input images and their corresponding vector drawing results. VectorDraw supports different abstraction levels by varying the number of strokes. A lower number of strokes produces a more abstract drawing and reveals more of the object's fundamental visual structure.
